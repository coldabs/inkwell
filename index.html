How the tools work

Advanced layer selection: pick a whole layer's strokes at once, then move, rotate, copy or delete them together with the selection bar under the page.

Stylus & mouse draw with real pressure. One finger draws with the tool you picked; drag with two fingers to scroll the page without putting the tool down. The Hand tool scrolls with one finger. A selected tool stays selected until you pick a different tool.

Drawing tools

Shapes: drag with Line, Rectangle, Ellipse or Arrow. Tick Fill shape to fill rectangles and ellipses. Tick Shape recogniser and a rough straight pen stroke snaps to a line, a closed loop snaps to an ellipse. Select: drag a box around strokes, then drag inside it to move them, or duplicate / delete them from the bar under the page. Text: tap the page to place typed text right on the paper — tap it again to edit, empty it to remove it. Insert image drops a picture on the page; drag it to move, drag its corner dot to resize. Zoom and page colour are under Page in the toolbar above.

Spline joins tapped points with a smooth curve. Compass draws a true circle from its centre. Lasso selects with a free loop; Vertical space pushes everything below a line down. Selections can be rotated in 15° steps, resized, flipped, copied and pasted across pages. Style gives pen and shapes dashed, dotted or dash-dot strokes; the Eraser deletes any whole stroke you touch — use Eraser options to switch it to cutting strokes or whiteout instead. The Size slider names each thickness in plain words and previews the stroke at its real width, so you can see how thick the next stroke will be before you draw it. Smooth is input stabilization for shaky hands, Snap to grid aligns shapes and moved selections, and the Set square is a draggable, rotatable guide. LaTeX equation places rendered maths on the page. The pen's eraser end or barrel button borrows the tool picked under Pen button. Load a .gpl palette to replace the colour swatches, and export any notebook as .xopp to open it in other handwriting apps — or open a .xopp here.
