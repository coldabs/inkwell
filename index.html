Using the tools

Stylus and mouse draw with pressure. One finger draws with the selected tool; two fingers scroll. Hand scrolls with one finger. With Pen only on, the pen draws and your finger scrolls instead.

Shapes drag with Line, Rectangle, Ellipse or Arrow. Select drag a box, then drag inside to move. Text tap the page to place typed text. Insert adds images, tables, stickies and equations.

Spline joins tapped points. Compass draws a true circle. Lasso selects with a free loop. Vertical space pushes content down. Selections can be rotated, resized, flipped, copied and pasted across pages.

Eraser removes whole strokes by default; switch to Cut or Whiteout in Pen options. Style gives strokes dashed, dotted or dash-dot lines. Load a .gpl palette to replace the swatches. Export a notebook as .xopp to open it in other handwriting apps.
