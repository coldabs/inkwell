Layers

Stylus & mouse draw with real pressure. With Finger = “Scrolls page”, one finger scrolls and never draws — no need to deselect the pen. Tap a selected tool again to put it down.

Drawing tools

Shapes: drag with Line, Rectangle, Ellipse or Arrow. Tick Fill shape to fill rectangles and ellipses. Tick Shape recogniser and a rough straight pen stroke snaps to a line, a closed loop snaps to an ellipse. Select: drag a box around strokes, then drag inside it to move them, or duplicate / delete them from the bar under the page. Text: tap the page to place typed text right on the paper — tap it again to edit, empty it to remove it. Insert image drops a picture on the page; drag it to move, drag its corner dot to resize. Zoom and page colour are in the toolbar above.
