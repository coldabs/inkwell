Why speed lies

From The Art of Slow Reading — sample included so every reader tool can be tried without importing a file.

Reading slowly is not reading less. It is noticing what speed hides: the turn of an argument, the weight of a word, the place where you stopped agreeing and started merely nodding. A page read once, carefully, stays with you longer than three pages skimmed in a hurry.

That is why Inkwell opens books in reading mode first. You scroll to read. When you want to write, you tap Annotate and the pen appears — your finger still scrolls, your stylus still draws, and the two never fight each other.

Try it: select any sentence and tap Highlight. Add a bookmark. Switch to Sepia or Night. Press Listen below and pick an accent — only a few, named, and only the ones that actually speak on this device.
